How to Play

Pick a card, then a square. If your card's number beats the touching side of an enemy card, it turns to your colour. Own the most cards when the board fills to win. Cards still in your hand count too. A is 10.

Spoils. One: winner takes one card. Margin: one card per point of difference. Claim: everyone keeps the cards they own on the board at the end. All: winner takes the whole hand.
